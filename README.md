# CarbonSustain

Decentralized carbon credit verification platform using ICP and Telegram Mini Apps.

🏆 **ETHDenver 2025 — Internet Computer Track, Third Prize**

---

## The Problem We Solve

### ❌ Greenwashing & Broken Carbon Markets
- 19 out of 20 Verra carbon credits are **phantom credits**
- Traditional MRV is **centralized, slow, and expensive**
- Companies face fines under SB 253 & SB 261 for unverifiable sustainability claims

### ✅ Our Solution: Decentralized, Verifiable Carbon Credits
CarbonSustain creates a **trustless MRV pipeline** using:
- Geofenced GPS validation
- AI-enhanced climate and weather data
- DAO-governed verification
- Smart contract–issued carbon credits

---

## Key Features

- **Telegram Mini App** — 1B+ users, instant MRV submission
- **Geofenced Verification** — prevents fraudulent reports
- **DAO-Governed Validation** — trustless approval system
- **AI + Weather Data Integration** — accurate carbon tracking
- **Gamified Leaderboard** — drives engagement and rewards
- **ICP Smart Contracts** — tamper-proof MRV records

---

## How It Works

1. **User submits data** through Telegram (photo + GPS)
2. **ICP canister** stores data immutably
3. **Geofencing** verifies location authenticity
4. **DAO votes** on submission validity
5. **Smart contracts distribute** carbon crypto credits
6. **Leaderboard updates** in real time
